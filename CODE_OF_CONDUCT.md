# Code of conduct

We work in one organization. Be direct, stay on the issue, and leave the codebase easier to operate than you found it.

## Expected

- Write decisions on the issue, not only in chat.
- Keep tenant data, tokens, and customer information out of issues, pull requests, and logs.
- Review the change you were asked to review.
- Disagree on the work, then record the decision and move on.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, personal attacks, or sharing someone's private information.
- Shipping a production change that has not been tested on the non-production environment, unless the operator explicitly asks for both.
- Bypassing tenant isolation, append-only ledger rules, or the admin email-domain check.
