# SalesArck

Loyalty software for merchants and the people who shop with them.

| Repository | What it is |
|---|---|
| [salesarck-monorepo](https://github.com/SalesArck/salesarck-monorepo) | Loyalty product: API, merchant and consumer portal, reward engine, and POS integrations (Square, Clover, Toast, Lighthouse). |
| [salesarck-documentation](https://github.com/SalesArck/salesarck-documentation) | Source of truth for product behavior and how the monorepo is implemented. Published at [salesarck-documentation.pages.dev](https://salesarck-documentation.pages.dev). |
| [salesarck-website](https://github.com/SalesArck/salesarck-website) | Marketing site at [salesarck.com](https://salesarck.com). |
| [salesarck-rewards](https://github.com/SalesArck/salesarck-rewards) | Separate multi-tenant loyalty platform. It does not share schema, business rules, or deployment with the monorepo. |

The live product is [loyalty.salesarck.com](https://loyalty.salesarck.com).
